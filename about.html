About Us

Born from a shared obsession with everything that has four wheels and a heartbeat.

We are the Council of Cars — a ragtag bunch of car enthusiasts who found each other through a shared love of engines, exhausts, and endlessly talking about cars we probably can't afford.

It started the way most great things do: a parking lot, a couple of cold drinks, and someone saying "nice ride." One conversation led to another, and before long we had a group chat, a running tab of weekend meetups, and an unspoken rule that no topic was off the table — as long as it had an engine.

Our members span the full spectrum of automotive culture. You'll find classic restorers elbow-deep in chrome and rust alongside daily-driver tuners who squeeze every last horsepower from their humble hatchbacks. We've got track-day addicts, off-road explorers, concours purists, and at least one person who genuinely believes their estate wagon is misunderstood performance art.

We don't discriminate by make, model, or country of origin. Whether you're obsessed with Italian drama, German precision, Japanese reliability, or old-school American muscle — if you love cars, you belong here.

The Council of Cars is whatever its members make it. Right now it's this site, a handful of group chats, and a growing calendar of meetups. Tomorrow? Who knows — but it'll definitely involve horsepower.

If you want to join, bring your car, bring your stories, and bring your opinions. Just be warned: the debates about manual vs. automatic never end, and they never will.
